## What this demo stores

This demo version keeps everything on your phone. It does not send data to a server.

- Mobile number you register with
- Name and profile photo (optional)
- A salted hash of your PIN (kept in the phone's secure storage; we never see your PIN)
- Demo transactions, savings goals, messages, nominee details (optional)
- Settings (language, fingerprint, privacy options)

## Permissions

- Camera: to scan QR codes and take a profile photo
- Photos: to choose a profile photo
- Fingerprint / face: to unlock the app (the phone does the check; we never get your fingerprint)

## Your choices

- Settings > Delete this account removes your data from the phone.
- Uninstalling the app also removes it.
- You can copy a backup code in Settings > Backup & restore. Anyone who has the code can read your
  data, so keep it private.

## When the real service starts

Before PayBD handles real money, this policy will be replaced. It will say what is sent to our
servers, why, how long we keep it, who we share it with (for example a licensed bank or payment
partner), and how you can ask us to delete it.
