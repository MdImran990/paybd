# Store listing drafts (demo build)

Keep the word "demo" in the listing until the app really moves money with a licence.

## English

**App name (max 30):** PayBD (Demo)

**Short description (max 80):**
Demo digital wallet: send money, pay bills, save and scan QR. No real money.

**Full description:**
PayBD is a demo of a digital wallet app. It shows how everyday payments can look and feel.
This build does NOT hold or move real money. All balances, transactions and OTP codes are
for testing only.

What you can try
- Register with a mobile number and a 5-digit PIN
- Send money, cash out, add money and recharge (demo)
- Pay bills, donate and pay education fees (demo)
- Save towards goals
- Scan and show QR codes
- Statement with PDF download, spending insights and limits
- Fingerprint unlock and screenshot protection
- English and Bangla

Your data stays on your phone in this demo.
